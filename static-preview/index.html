What they do

The business

capability
Makes and sells across five appliance categories under Northwind, and distributes a licensed premium brand in its home market.
offer
Kitchen and home appliances across five categories at value prices, plus a semi-premium fitted range. Published prices run Rs 4,000 to Rs 40,000.
buyer
Households buying a kitchen appliance, reached three ways: general-trade outlets where the shopkeeper chooses the shelf, marketplaces where the platform owns the relationship, and own stores where the company does.
monetization
FY2026 revenue Rs 1,200.0 crore at a 36.0% gross margin, converting to a 10.0% EBITDA margin and a Rs 30.0 crore profit.
pricing power
In one category, realised price per unit rose about 9% while volume grew 10%. Whether that is price or mix is not separable from public data.
growth initiatives
250 own stores across 18 states, 50 added during the year, contributing 6.0% of revenue.

How to find out cheaply

Bound
Split FY2026 that category value and volume by stock-keeping unit to separate a price effect from a mix effect, then do the same for every other category. Where a genuine price effect appears, take a 5% list increase into one category for one quarter. It is a yes if unit volume falls by less than 5% and realised price per unit holds the increase.
Bound
Take FY2025 and FY2026 and break the gross-to-EBITDA gap into advertising and trade spend, freight and warehousing, own-retail running cost, and everything else. For each line, compute the change as a percentage of the change in revenue. Any line rising faster than revenue is the answer. It is a yes to act if a single line accounts for more than a third of the incremental gap.
Bound
Before any arithmetic, fix in writing how central advertising, brand spend and warehousing are apportioned between own retail, trade and marketplaces, because that choice decides the answer. Then for FY2026 compute, per channel: revenue at the price it is actually booked, gross margin realised, allocated operating cost, and inventory plus receivables tied up. Express each as contribution per rupee of capital employed. It is a yes to accelerate own retail if it returns 1.5 times the trade channel on that measure, and a yes to slow it if it returns less.
Bound
For the last 24 months, pull order value, gross margin and reorder frequency for each of the 10 countries. Rank them. It is a yes to concentrate if the top three markets carry more than 70% of export contribution, which would mean the other eleven are consuming attention for very little.
Bound
Take own-retail and own-website transactions for 24 months, the only channels where identity exists, and count how many buyers purchased in more than one category and at what interval. It is a yes to invest in identity if more than 20% bought a second category, because that rate applied to the other 92.5% of revenue is the prize.
Bound
Publish three matched chimney-and-hob sets at a set price and place them with 20 kitchen dealers and modular-kitchen specifiers. Over one quarter, measure attach rate, the share of chimney sales that carry a hob, against the preceding quarter. It is a yes if attach rate rises by 15 percentage points or more without gross margin percentage on the pair falling.

How these numbers were arrived at

Every figure is either a reported result or arithmetic on one, with the working shown. THIS IS DUMMY DATA.

CALC-1

The gap between gross margin and EBITDA. (36.0% - 10.0%) x Rs 1,200.0 crore = Rs 312 crore.

CALC-2

Own-retail revenue. 6.0% x Rs 1,200.0 crore = Rs 72.0 crore.

CALC-3

Revenue per own store. Rs 72.0 crore / 250 stores = Rs 28.8 lakh per year, about Rs 2.4 lakh per month.

CALC-4

Implied price move. 1.20 / 1.10 = 1.09, about 9% more per unit. Limit: mix produces identical arithmetic.
